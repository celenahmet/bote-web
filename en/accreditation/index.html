Accreditation

Accreditation

Accreditation; It refers to the evaluation and external quality assurance process that measures whether a higher education program meets the academic and field-specific standards predetermined by an accreditation body in a certain field.

Some accredited organizations in Turkey

Mühendislik Eğitim Programları Değerlendirme ve Akreditasyon derneği (MÜDEK)
Öğretmenlik Eğitim Programları Değerlendirme ve Akreditasyon Derneği (EPDAD)

Accreditation in Turkey

The Higher Education Quality Board is responsible for the authorization and recognition activities of accreditation bodies in Turkey. The process of authorization of national accreditation bodies and recognition of international accreditation bodies is carried out within the scope of the principles and criteria determined by YÖKAK.

While ABET accreditation is found in some engineering programs of leading universities such as METU, Hacettepe, İTÜ and Boğaziçi, there are many universities with MÜDEK accreditation. EPDAD accreditation is also applied as teaching accreditation.
