Internship

Internship is the preparatory period in which the first step into business life is taken, providing the opportunity to put the theoretical knowledge acquired throughout education into practice. The internship period, which is also the first stop of career planning; It gives the opportunity to get to know the sector chosen to make a career. To have preliminary information about the institution by researching the institution where the internship will be carried out; It is important for easy adaptation to the institution and for the internship period to be productive. It should not be forgotten that the first door to business life can be opened with a productive internship period. Because interns who are successful within the institution may have the opportunity to stay at the institution after the internship period or to get a job by being referred to a different institution by the institution.

Compulsory Internship at CEIT

It is compulsory to do an internship as a teaching practice in the spring and fall semesters of the 4th grade in schools. While CEIT students become ready for teaching skills through internship, they continue their theoretical and practical training in their own faculties.

Volunteer Internship at CEIT

Students can gain experience in the business world by doing voluntary internships by applying to companies in the relevant sector in many fields such as software, design and teaching. Recruitment can be made according to the companies' intern needs. Students who are not seniors can apply for such internship practices to improve themselves.
