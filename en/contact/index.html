Computer and Instructional Technologies Education

The year is 1998, education faculties are being restructured. In a sense, the cards are being redistributed. With the digitalizing world, it is inevitable that computer-related departments will become popular and the CEIT department is established.

The first graduates were given in 2002. Curriculum changes were made in 2006 and 2018. CEIT graduates continue to be guides and pioneers in the sector because they are knowledgeable in every field.

"The best teacher is life. He always teaches us something without getting tired, if only we can understand it."

Ahmet ÇELEN

I have been interested in mathematics and computers since I was young. I founded a sole proprietorship when I was still a minor and ran it for a few years. I had to close it because my family told me to study for the university exam.

During the preparation process for the exam, I prepared both myself and my competitors to support them during the pandemic. I broadcast mathematics on my YouTube channel and shared it as files. I was accepted to Hacettepe University in 2023.
