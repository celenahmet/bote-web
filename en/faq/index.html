Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

You can find frequently asked questions here. You can look at the questions and answers to learn more about the episode. It contains questions that are recommended to be read before making a choice. You can contact us for detailed information.

Who is CEIT suitable for?
CEIT department is suitable for anyone who is interested in computers, loves learning and teaching, and can keep up with the digitalizing world.
Is it possible to only teach at CEIT?
They can work in that position by advancing in many fields such as software, hardware, artificial intelligence and design. Apart from teaching, they can work in positions such as IT in public offices.
Are there double majors and minors at CEIT?
It is possible to complete a double major (double diploma) by teaching in the numerical group. Some universities offer both double major and minor opportunities in departments such as computer engineering.
What is the status of CEIT in university rankings??
The CEIT department, which is intertwined with computers and technology, is in leading universities and is making progress in the digital field.
Are there exchange programs such as Erasmus at CEIT?
Exchange programs are possible through agreements between universities. Since the CEIT department is located in leading universities, CEIT students can benefit from exchange programs such as Erasmus and Farabi.

What are the advantages of studying at CEIT?

In addition to having knowledge in every field related to computers, their ability to be a guide and pioneer in many subjects can be considered an advantage, as they also have knowledge of educational sciences. Graduates who choose and specialize in the field they want to advance in can become sought-after business people.

Why is CEIT only available at leading universities??
Such an attitude may have been followed because quality and quantity are generally inversely proportional.
Is CEIT Technical Teaching?
They are not included in the technical teaching group of the CEIT Department.
What are the disadvantages of studying CEIT?
Since she has knowledge in many fields, it may be considered a disadvantage if specialization in one field is not preferred.
How are CEIT Department appointments?
While teachers produce fewer graduates each year, their appointments are proportionally fewer. However, CEIT graduates can work in positions such as IT Manager in public institutions.
Can CEIT Graduates do the software work done by Engineers?
There are differences between engineering education in terms of curricula and accreditations, but if the student improves himself in that field, it is possible, although it is not an official engineer title. If there is qualification, it is possible to work depending on the principles and attitudes of the companies.
